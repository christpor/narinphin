# Reference-Informed Refinement Specification

## Chosen Direction

The revised portfolio will retain its quiet scholarly character but borrow four interaction principles from the reference site: an elevated, compact navigation surface; a **real, explicit language control**; a short visual hero with two actions rather than multiple paragraphs; and content organised into purposeful, scannable collections. It will not imitate the reference site’s branded mosaic or its technology-community content.

## Language Control

The header will include a visible `Languages` icon with the active label **ខ្មែរ** in English mode and **EN** in Khmer mode. The control will persist the preference locally, update the document language attribute, and immediately translate navigation, page labels, hero titles, primary calls to action, and the contact entry message. Long professional details remain concise English reference material until Narin supplies or approves complete Khmer translations; in Khmer mode they will be visually de-emphasised rather than presented as a false full translation.

## Content Density

The homepage will reduce to a short career identity, three evidence markers, two CTAs, a compact route collection, and one small public-learning card. The long vertical “wall” of copy will be avoided by keeping complete professional history within `Journey`, expertise detail within `Expertise`, and the professional profile within `About`. Each of those pages will use an optional progressive disclosure element for secondary context rather than showing all explanatory prose at once.

## Motion and Navigation

Desktop Lenis remains on, but content will use fewer, more deliberate reveals. The header will become a white elevated bar that settles into a lightly translucent state after scrolling. The mobile menu will open as a contained elevated panel with the language switch at the top, distinct routes, and no background text collision. Navigation uses short opacity-and-transform transitions and respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Copy Corrections

Public copy will use **Open Gate Center for Education** rather than the concatenated “OpenGateCenter”. It will describe Narin as a former English Lecturer at Western University from 2007–2012 rather than imply current employment. The public site will omit phone numbers, reference names, and unconfirmed or overly granular performance claims from the CV.
